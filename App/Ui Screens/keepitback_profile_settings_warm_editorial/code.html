Help & Frequently Asked
How do I save a link or reel? expand_more

Share directly from Instagram, TikTok, or Safari via the native iOS Share Sheet, or paste any URL into the center ‘+’ Quick Save tab on your lower navigation bar.

Why didn’t all my Instagram Saved Reels import? expand_more

Instagram API limits retrieval to publicly accessible reels saved while connected. Private or expired audio stories cannot be archived.

How do I disconnect a social account? expand_more

Tap Connected Accounts above to unlink any platform at any time. Once disconnected, KeepItBack immediately stops reading saved items.

How do I delete an item from my library? expand_more

Swipe left on any saved item in your Library or folios and confirm delete. This removes the cache and record from your personal archive.
